Generate only soft white cumulus clouds with wispy translucent edges in diffuse daylight, isolated on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Broad horizontal composition, approximately 1536 by 1024. No sky rectangle, no landscape, no ground, no sun, no rain, no text, no symbols, no drop shadow. Feathery clouds only; retain alpha around and through thin wisps.
```
